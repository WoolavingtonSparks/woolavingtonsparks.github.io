What we're building

A fresh start for youth football in Woolavington.

Woolavington Football Club already has a men's team and a women's team. Sparks is the youth side of that same club — not a separate organisation.

Our hope is to give local children somewhere to enjoy football in their own village, with more age groups and teams as we grow. We're at the beginning of that journey, and that's something we're proud to be open about.

Useful to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Woolavington Sparks a separate football club?

No. Sparks is the youth section of Woolavington Football Club, alongside its men's and women's sections.

What if my child has never played football before?

That's absolutely fine. Little Sparks is designed to be a friendly introduction to football with lots of games, movement and fun.

Do we have to pay straight away?

No. New children can try Little Sparks before joining. We’ll explain when their £10 monthly subscription will start, particularly if they join partway through a month.

What happens if my child wants to stop?

There are no contracts, notice periods or cancellation charges. Simply let us know they won't be continuing.

When will more age groups start?

We're aiming to introduce a Reception-year session early in 2027, then gradually expand further as interest, volunteers and resources allow.

Do we need Spond before attending?

No. Simply contact the club about a taster session first. If your child enjoys it and you decide to join, we’ll send a private Spond invitation. Parents then register as guardians and add their children as players.
